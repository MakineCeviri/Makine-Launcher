Version 0.2.4 — Makine, the community inside the launcher, and refreshed pages

• Makine: your companion, in his own corner on the home page and at the foot of the sidebar elsewhere. He looks around while your games are found, gathers light while a patch downloads, cheers when it is installed and tells you when something goes wrong. Choose his place and size, or turn him off, in Settings > Appearance > Makine.
• Community: the makineceviri.org community wall inside the launcher. Sign in with a 6-digit code sent to your e-mail, or with Discord, to post, reply and vote.
• New look: My Library, All Games (with a list view), How it works, About and What's New were redesigned; the splash and welcome screens follow the launcher's look.
• Installing large patches such as Elden Ring no longer freezes the launcher first; if a folder inside the game was picked, the right folder is found.
• When a patch's own installer fails, the launcher now says why.
