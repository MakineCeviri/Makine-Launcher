Version 0.3.0 — Makine Live

• Makine Live is built in: it reads a game's English subtitle and writes the Turkish one over the game. Nothing is read before you press Start; images never leave your PC, only the recognised text goes to the server.
• Play GTA VI on PS5 or Xbox with Turkish subtitles: PS Remote Play, chiaki, Asobi, the Xbox app, Greenlight or a capture card. (GTA VI is named only to identify the game; not affiliated with Rockstar Games.)
• Subtitles where you want them: on this screen, on your TV (Makine TV) or in your phone's browser; plus a separate source for OBS.
• Readable subtitles: dark band, outline and shadow; size, lines, colour and position with a live preview.
• Plugins in every build; each runs in its own process and starts on first use.
